Wrapper behavior:
  The auto controller is outside the Rust daemon. It detects foreground apps,
  classifies them, and writes ordinary upstream-compatible KDL. It does not
  replace affinity/cpuset/sched/nice/uclamp/relock/hot-reload implementation;
  those remain implemented by the original threadctl-rs binary.
